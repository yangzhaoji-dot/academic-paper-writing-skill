# Evaluation rubric

Compare the structured pipeline against a direct "write this section" baseline.

Score or inspect the following dimensions separately.

## Semantic quality

- factual faithfulness;
- coverage of the supplied research content;
- claim-evidence alignment;
- calibrated claim strength;
- absence of invented citations or results.

## Narrative quality

- clarity of the central research question;
- visibility of the actual novelty;
- causal/argumentative continuity;
- whether each paragraph advances the story;
- whether experiments answer questions established earlier.

## Language quality

- semantic repetition;
- excessive explicit transitions;
- generic academic filler;
- sentence-pattern monotony;
- local coherence and reference chains;
- preservation of technical meaning during naturalization.

## Editability

Change one upstream claim or result and test whether downstream prose can be updated without reconstructing the paper from scratch.

A successful architecture should improve not only prose quality but also controllability and revision locality.
