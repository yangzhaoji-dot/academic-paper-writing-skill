# Introduction module: Context

## Purpose
Establish the research setting and the object the paper studies.

## Required inputs
- problem setting
- relevant capability or paradigm
- terminology needed by later claims

## Must accomplish
- give the reader enough context to understand the later gap;
- avoid broad historical surveys unless they are needed for the argument.

## Avoid
- generic "rapid progress" openings with no direct relation to the paper;
- claiming importance only through adjectives;
- introducing the method before the problem is visible.
