# Introduction module: Consequence

## Purpose
Explain why the identified limitation matters operationally, statistically, conceptually, or empirically.

## Required inputs
- limitation
- mechanism or evidence connecting the limitation to a consequence

## Must accomplish
- make the motivation causal or logical rather than purely rhetorical;
- distinguish demonstrated consequences from hypothesized ones.

## Avoid
- inventing a failure mode merely to make the gap appear consequential.
