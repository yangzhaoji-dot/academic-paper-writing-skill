# Introduction module: Contributions

## Purpose
Compress the paper's distinct contributions after the narrative has established why they matter.

## Required inputs
- primary claims
- method
- evidence

## Must accomplish
- each contribution should add a distinct function: formulation, method, evidence, analysis, or resource;
- use calibrated verbs that match claim strength.

## Avoid
- repeating the same contribution with different wording;
- including speculative implications as demonstrated contributions;
- generic "extensive experiments show effectiveness" without saying what was tested.
