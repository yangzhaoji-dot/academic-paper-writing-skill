# Introduction module: Limitation

## Purpose
Identify the specific restriction, missing variable, or fixed assumption that motivates the work.

## Required inputs
- existing paradigm
- target claim
- evidence or literature support for the limitation

## Must answer
- what is fixed, omitted, or constrained?
- why is that restriction relevant to the target problem?
- what scope can be defended?

## Avoid
- generic statements that existing methods are "limited";
- implying empirical failure if only a conceptual restriction has been established.
